Privacy notice.

What we collect when you use this website or register for the webinar, why we collect it, and the choices available to you.

Last updated 1 August 2026 Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia)

Applies to this website and the online registration form

This notice explains how AIMST University, through its Faculty of Dentistry ("AIMST", "we", "us"), handles personal data in connection with the webinar Graduation to Global Practice: Your Dental Career Journey, held online on 19–20 September 2026.

We process personal data in line with the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) and, where applicable, other laws that apply to participants joining from outside Malaysia.

1. Data we collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Identity and contact data — name, email address, telephone number, institution or place of work.
  • Registration data — participant category (student, alumnus, practitioner), session preferences, dietary or accessibility requirements you choose to share.
  • Payment data — confirmation of payment, transaction reference and amount. We do not store full card details on this website; payments are handled by the designated payment channel.
  • Communication data — messages sent to the secretariat through the contact form, email or messaging applications.
  • Technical data — browser type, device type, approximate location derived from your IP address, and pages visited, collected in aggregate for website security and performance.

2. How we use your data

We use personal data to:

  • process your registration and confirm your place at the webinar;
  • issue receipts, joining instructions and certificates of attendance;
  • respond to enquiries and provide participant support;
  • send essential updates about the programme, schedule or access links;
  • improve the programme and the website experience;
  • meet accounting, audit and legal obligations.

With your consent, we may also send information about future Faculty of Dentistry events. You can withdraw that consent at any time by replying to any message or contacting the secretariat.

3. Lawful basis

We rely on your consent, the performance of the agreement created when you register, our legitimate interests in running an educational event, and compliance with legal obligations. Where consent is the basis, you may withdraw it without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

4. Sharing your data

We share personal data only where necessary, and only with:

  • authorised staff and committee members of the Faculty of Dentistry and AIMST University;
  • partner organisations involved in delivering the webinar (Virtual Clinical Training and Dental Diary Malaysia), limited to what they need to perform their role;
  • technology providers that host the registration form, webinar platform, email or website, under appropriate confidentiality obligations;
  • authorities or advisers where required by law or to protect the rights and safety of participants.

We do not sell personal data. Where a service provider processes data outside Malaysia, we take reasonable steps to ensure a comparable standard of protection.

5. Retention

We keep registration and attendance records for as long as needed to administer the webinar, issue certificates and satisfy audit and accounting requirements — normally up to seven years after the event. Enquiry correspondence is kept for up to two years unless a longer period is required. Data that is no longer needed is securely deleted or anonymised.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold personal data about you and provide access to it;
  • correct data that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date;
  • withdraw consent or object to certain processing, including direct marketing;
  • limit or cease processing in specific circumstances;
  • explain how your data has been used.

To exercise a right, contact the secretariat at aimstdentalcareer@gmail.com. We may ask for proof of identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within the period required by law.

7. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It stores a single preference — your choice of light or dark theme — in your browser's local storage. That value stays on your device, is not sent to us, and can be cleared through your browser settings.

Embedded third-party services (for example the registration form or fonts) may set their own cookies or process technical data under their own privacy policies. We link only to services we consider appropriate for this purpose.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including access controls, encrypted connections and restricted sharing, to protect personal data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No online transmission is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive clinical or patient information through the contact form.

9. Children and patient data

The webinar is intended for dental students and practitioners aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Please do not include patient-identifiable information in any message to the secretariat.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this notice to reflect changes in the programme or the law. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.

Questions about this notice, or a request relating to your personal data, can be sent to:

  • Email: aimstdentalcareer@gmail.com
  • Telephone: +60 17-851 1044
  • Post: Faculty of Dentistry, AIMST University, Jalan Bedong–Semeling, 08100 Bedong, Kedah, Malaysia

This notice is a working draft prepared for the webinar and should be reviewed by AIMST University before publication.
