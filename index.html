About the webinar

A bridge between graduation and the world stage.

“Graduation to Global Practice: Your Dental Career Journey” brings together distinguished international and national speakers to support dental students, recent graduates, Dental Officers and early-career practitioners as they move from university training into professional practice — and towards wider career opportunities.

The programme is built as a forward-looking platform for dental practice in Malaysia, international career pathways and entrepreneurship. Every session is designed to answer the questions that rarely appear in a lecture hall.

Professional readiness involves much more than completing a dental degree.
  • International and national speakers from diverse backgrounds
  • Interactive Q&A sessions to guide participants
  • Clear routes into Malaysia, the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore and Hong Kong

Meet the organisers

Why it matters

The transition nobody fully prepares you for.

Moving from graduate to practitioner affects career mobility and patient safety. Graduates follow diverse paths — government service, private practice, academia, hospital dentistry, public health, the military, corporate healthcare or overseas practice — and each one carries different registration systems, licensing exams, language requirements, immigration rules and scope-of-practice expectations.

Early planning matters. Mapping a desired pathway against its specific requirements reduces barriers later. Practical habits — keeping CPD records, maintaining indemnity cover, tracking APC deadlines and finding mentorship — make the difference between a plan and a career.

  • MDC registration — professional registration with the Malaysian Dental Council.
  • APC renewal — Annual Practising Certificate requirements and timelines.
  • CPD hours — continuing professional development and its documentation.
  • Ethics & conduct — MDC ethical codes and professional practice standards.
  • Regulatory compliance — health legislation and infection-control guidelines.
  • International readiness — exams, language and immigration requirements.

Scope & key themes

Three pillars, one clear direction.

The programme is organised around the decisions that shape a dental career — at home, in business and abroad.

01

Preparing for dental practice in Malaysia

The knowledge and skills needed to begin practice confidently and responsibly inside the Malaysian healthcare system. Professional readiness involves much more than a degree — registration, regulation and conduct sit at the centre of safe practice.

02

Career development, entrepreneurship & future practice

  • The transition from clinician to practice owner or dental entrepreneur.
  • Opportunities, expectations and progression within government and military dental services.
03

Overseas registration examination

Career opportunities beyond Malaysia, and the expanding roles of dentists as innovators, practice owners, educators, researchers and global healthcare leaders.

Webinar objectives

What you will leave with.

01 / Practice

Begin practice with confidence

Understand the knowledge and skills required to begin dental practice confidently and responsibly in Malaysia.

02 / Regulation

Know the rules that protect patients

Recognise the professional, ethical, legal and regulatory requirements governing practice — MDC registration, APC, CPD hours, patient safety and scope of practice.

03 / Specialties

See specialty postings up close

Gain an overview of Dental Officer responsibilities, case management, teamwork, emergency care, consultation and referral pathways.

04 / Ownership

Build a practice, not just a clinic

Understand planning, financing, regulatory compliance, staffing, infection control and quality assurance when establishing and managing a practice.

05 / Global

Map an international pathway

Gain introductory knowledge of overseas registration, qualification assessments, language requirements, licensing exams and supervised practice.

06 / Confidence

Move from graduate to globally prepared professional

Receive practical guidance, realistic expectations and inspiration to transition smoothly into a confident, ethical and globally prepared career.

Expected outcomes

Practical outcomes, not just inspiration.

By the close of day two, participants should be able to:

  • Explain the official regulatory and licensing pathways for practising dentistry within Malaysia.
  • Demonstrate a clear understanding of clinical responsibilities across specialty postings.
  • Identify career paths aligned with personal professional interests and clinical aspirations.
  • Plan the setup and administration of a private dental clinic using sound business principles.
  • Apply effective problem-solving strategies to professional, ethical and workplace challenges.
  • Compare international licensing pathways and examination requirements across destination countries.
  • Select an overseas registration pathway aligned with long-term global career objectives.
  • Appreciate the value of entrepreneurship, lifelong learning and continuous professional development.
  • Transition smoothly from graduate status into a competent, ethical and globally prepared practitioner.
