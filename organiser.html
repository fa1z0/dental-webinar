Built by clinicians, for the next generation.

Host institution

AIMST University — Faculty of Dentistry

AIMST University is a leading private university in Malaysia, known for its strong research culture and academic excellence across the health sciences. Its Faculty of Dentistry is committed to producing socially responsible, clinically competent dental professionals and is proud to host “Graduation to Global Practice”. The initiative provides guidance on licensing, registration pathways and professional requirements for practising in destination countries such as the UK, New Zealand and the USA.

Global practice pathways Academic excellence Licensing guidance
E-learning partner

Virtual Clinical Training (VCT)

VCT is a Malaysian online continuing education platform tailored for dental students and practitioners. It offers interactive virtual sessions, case discussions and specialised dental webinars — including exam mastery coaching for the MFDS and clinical webinars — to bridge theoretical knowledge and practical patient care.

Interactive webinars Case discussions MFDS exam coaching
CPD & training provider

Dental Diary Malaysia

Dental Diary Malaysia (operating as Dental Diary Academy) is a continuing professional development and training provider for dental practitioners in Kuala Lumpur. Founded by Dr. Vinesh Raj, the academy offers hands-on clinical courses, exam coaching (such as MFDS/MFD) and specialised training webinars.
